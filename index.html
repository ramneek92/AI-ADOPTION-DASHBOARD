Operating Plan · First Six Months

Ramneek's 90 Day Roadmap

You set the strategy. I run it on the ground.

A large organization doesn't have one way of working — it has a dozen, each department with its own tools, habits, and reasons to resist. The work isn't a mandate from the top; it's standardization earned team by team, with AI adoption and a real ROI baseline riding on the same rails. This is the playbook I'd run at any company — not just one process, but a centralized way of working across every department.

What This Plan Is Built For

  • Fragmented tooling & process — different tools and workflows across departments, no shared taxonomy or single source of truth.
  • Resistance — teams and leaders wary of yet another change program.
  • AI in motion — a bootcamp and Claude rollout that needs measurement and guardrails.
  • The ask — a partner in the weeds so the CTO stays on strategy.
